## Technical interview explanation

I used MetroPT-3 to study how unusual pressure, motor current and temperature patterns could support maintenance review. I preserved the original model results, then separated February training, March calibration and April development, with an additional earlier chronological development fold. Features use trailing observations and reset across outages. I compared robust deviation, regularized Mahalanobis distance, descriptive regime-conditioned references and reduced-feature Isolation Forest. Empirical calibration avoids assuming Gaussian control limits. The policy selector balances a predefined workload target, episode burden, temporal stability and complexity, rejects silence, and limits the influence of a single reported event. I froze this decision before scoring the already inspected May–September benchmark. The selected covariance model detected one of three benchmark intervals; the alternative forest detected two. Neither result proves reliable future failure prediction. Event coverage, warning definitions, reproducible artifacts and 62 passing tests make that limitation visible and testable.
